### 1.3 Ramadan and Idul Fitri 2027 (expected)

- **1 Ramadan 1448 H: about Monday 8 Feb 2027.** The MABIMS, KHGT/Turki and Ummul Qura criteria all give 8 Feb (BRIN and media reporting).
- **1 Syawal 1448 H: 10 Mar 2027** by MABIMS (the government criterion, matching the SKB). It is 9 Mar 2027 by KHGT/Ummul Qura, which Muhammadiyah is likely to follow [UNVERIFIED for Muhammadiyah's own decision]. The government's final date comes from the Sidang Isbat, held around 29 Sya'ban.

### 1.4 How businesses operate around Lebaran

- **THR (Tunjangan Hari Raya):** must be paid **no later than H-7** (7 days before the holiday), **in full, not in installments**. Basis: PP 36/2021 and Permenaker 6/2016. For 2026 it was set by SE Menaker No. M/3/HK.04.00/III/2026, and a new SE is issued each year. For Lebaran 10 Mar 2027 the deadline is about 3 Mar 2027. Employers are encouraged to pay earlier. SMB cash planning should therefore reserve about 1 month of payroll in mid-to-late Ramadan (the amount rule for staff with 12+ months of service is 1 month's wage [UNVERIFIED for this pass, standard Permenaker 6/2016 rule]).
- **Cuti bersama in the private sector is optional.** Menaker says it is "fakultatif". If a worker takes it, the day is deducted from annual leave (cuti tahunan). If they work that day, annual leave is not reduced and they get normal pay for a working day.
- **Couriers:** in 2026, JNE operated through Lebaran with branch-level hours. J&T operated "365 days" with short hours on the holiday itself (21 Mar 12:00–18:00, 22 Mar 10:00–16:00) and advised "safe send" cutoffs of 16 Mar (within Java) and 10–13 Mar (outside Java). SiCepat usually closes on H (the day of Lebaran) and its last pickup was about H-3. Expect similar patterns in 2027. Advise customers to order goods about **2 weeks before Lebaran** for outside Java.
- **Common practice [UNVERIFIED, general practice, not sourced]:**
  - Mudik: staff go home from about H-7 to H-3, and many small shops, warungs, clinics and suppliers close from about H-2 to H+5/H+7.
  - Factories and wholesale suppliers pause.
  - Demand peaks before Lebaran (kue kering/hampers, clothing, parcels, groceries).
  - F&B in Muslim-majority areas shifts to buka puasa / sahur hours and often uses curtains during the day.
  - Payment collection slows after Lebaran.
  - Plan restocking and invoices before the break.
  - Expect a slow restart after it.
- QRIS and e-wallet volume spikes before Lebaran. GoPay reported QRIS transactions rising 25x ahead of Lebaran 2026.

### 1.5 Nyepi (Bali)

- 2026: Thu 19 Mar, 06:00 to 06:00 next day. **2027: Mon 8 Mar**, which is also the day before the Lebaran cuti bersama.
- On Bali for 24 hours:
  - **Ngurah Rai airport closes.** In 2026 about 400 flights were cancelled.
  - Mobile internet, TV and radio are shut down.
  - Shops, entertainment and roads close, and pecalang (village security) enforce the rules.
  - Large hotel restaurants operate for guests only.
  - No deliveries, no outbound messaging campaigns, and staff cannot work.
- Plan Bali shipments and campaigns around the day before (Pengerupukan, ogoh-ogoh parades in the evening) and Nyepi itself.

### 1.6 Regional / religious days (not national)

- **Bali:** Galungan and Kuningan follow a 210-day Pawukon cycle. 2026: Galungan Wed 17 Jun, Kuningan Sat 27 Jun. Next: Galungan **about 13 Jan 2027**, Kuningan **about 23 Jan 2027** (computed from the 210-day cycle, [UNVERIFIED]). They are set as libur fakultatif / regional by the Bali provincial government: schools and offices close, and private businesses are not obliged to. Saraswati and Pagerwesi are also observed in Bali [UNVERIFIED dates].
- Other provinces and regencies set local holidays, for example a regional anniversary (HUT Kota/Provinsi) or local religious days [UNVERIFIED, check the local Pemda].
- Election days (Pilkada/Pemilu) are declared national holidays when held [UNVERIFIED, none known scheduled for the rest of 2026–2027].

## 2. Time zones and formatting

### 2.1 Time zones (no daylight saving)

| Zone                              | UTC   | Provinces                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **WIB** (Waktu Indonesia Barat)   | UTC+7 | All of Sumatra (Aceh, Sumut, Sumbar, Riau, Kepri, Jambi, Bengkulu, Sumsel, Babel, Lampung), all of Java (Banten, DKI Jakarta, Jabar, Jateng, DIY, Jatim), **Kalimantan Barat, Kalimantan Tengah** |
| **WITA** (Waktu Indonesia Tengah) | UTC+8 | **Bali**, NTB, NTT, **Kalimantan Selatan, Kalimantan Timur, Kalimantan Utara**, all Sulawesi provinces (Sulut, Gorontalo, Sulteng, Sulbar, Sulsel, Sultra)                                        |
| **WIT** (Waktu Indonesia Timur)   | UTC+9 | Maluku, Maluku Utara, all Papua provinces (Papua, Papua Barat, Papua Barat Daya, Papua Tengah, Papua Pegunungan, Papua Selatan)                                                                   |

- WIB is 1 hour behind WITA and 2 hours behind WIT. Kalimantan is split: Kalbar and Kalteng use WIB, the rest use WITA. The province-to-zone mapping outside Kalimantan is standard [lightly verified].
- Always state the zone in messages and schedules ("pukul 14.00 WIB"). Government and courier announcements are often in "waktu setempat" (local time).

### 2.2 Number, date and currency formatting

- **Rupiah:** `Rp` with **no space and no period**, a period as the thousands separator and a comma for decimals. Examples: `Rp1.250.000`, `Rp1.250.000,00`, `Rp2.500,25`. Wrong forms: `Rp 1.250.000`, `Rp.1.250.000`, `IDR1,250,000.00`. Spaced `Rp 1.250.000` is very common in casual and commercial use, but EYD/PUEBI says no space. "Rp1,2 juta", "Rp15 rb" and "Rp15k" are used informally.
- Words: ribu (rb), juta (jt), miliar (M), triliun (T). For example, "Rp2,5 jt" = Rp2.500.000. "Ceban" = Rp10.000, "goceng" = Rp5.000, "gopek" = Rp500 (slang).
- Amounts on invoices and kwitansi are also written in words ("terbilang"): "Satu juta dua ratus lima puluh ribu rupiah" [UNVERIFIED as a legal requirement; it is standard practice].
- **Dates:** `dd/mm/yyyy` or `dd-mm-yyyy` (01/11/2026), long form "1 November 2026", with the day name "Minggu, 1 November 2026". Months: Januari, Februari, Maret, April, Mei, Juni, Juli, Agustus, September, Oktober, November, Desember. Days: Senin, Selasa, Rabu, Kamis, Jumat, Sabtu, Minggu.
- **Time:** 24-hour with a period, "14.30 WIB" (a colon "14:30" is also seen). "Pagi" (morning), "siang" (about 11–15), "sore" (about 15–18), "malam" (evening/night).
- Hijri dates are written like "1 Syawal 1447 H". Relative holiday days: H-7 = 7 days before, H+3 = 3 days after.
- Common abbreviations:
  - Jl. = jalan
  - No. = nomor
  - Gg. = gang
  - Kel. = kelurahan, Kec. = kecamatan, Kab. = kabupaten
  - Prov. = provinsi
  - Tbk = listed company, PT = Perseroan Terbatas, CV = Commanditaire Vennootschap, UD = Usaha Dagang
  - s.d. / s/d = sampai dengan
  - a.n. = atas nama
  - u.p. = untuk perhatian
  - dll = dan lain-lain
  - dsb = dan sebagainya
  - yth. = yang terhormat
  - tgl = tanggal
  - pcs = pieces
  - ttd = tanda tangan
  - cap = stamp
  - rek. = rekening
  - norek = nomor rekening

## 3. Payments

### 3.1 QRIS (Quick Response Code Indonesian Standard)

- **Registration:** free. The merchant registers with a licensed PJP (Penyelenggara Jasa Pembayaran), either:
  - a bank: BRImerchant, Livin' Merchant (Mandiri), Merchant BCA, etc.
  - a non-bank: GoPay/GoBiz, DANA Bisnis, OVO, ShopeePay, etc.
- Typical requirements: owner's KTP/NIK, active phone and email, a photo of the business, and a bank account for a bank PJP. Both static (fixed print) and dynamic (amount-specific) QR codes exist. One QRIS accepts payment from any bank or e-wallet app.
- **MDR (Merchant Discount Rate), set by BI. Effective 1 Oct 2026, for all QRIS types (static, dynamic, API):**

| Merchant category                            | ≤ Rp100.000 | Rp100.001–Rp500.000 | > Rp500.000 |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------- | ----------- |
| Usaha Mikro (UMI)                            | 0%          | 0%                  | 0,3%        |
| Usaha Kecil / Menengah / Besar (UKE/UME/UBE) | 0%          | 0,7%                | 0,7%        |
| Pendidikan (education)                       | 0%          | 0,6%                | 0,6%        |
| SPBU (fuel stations)                         | 0%          | 0,4%                | 0,4%        |
| G2P / bansos                                 | 0%          | 0%                  | 0%          |
| P2G (taxes, passports, non-profit donations) | 0%          | 0%                  | 0%          |
| BLU & PSO                                    | 0%          | 0%                  | 0%          |

- The MDR is charged to the merchant. **Merchants must not add the MDR onto the customer's bill** [UNVERIFIED for this pass, long-standing BI rule].
- Before 1 Oct 2026, UKE/UME/UBE paid 0,7% on every amount. The UMI 0% ≤Rp500.000 rule has been in place since 15 Mar 2025.
- **Limits:** the QRIS nominal limit is **Rp10 juta per transaction**. CPM (customer-presented QRIS Bayar) was raised to Rp10 juta from 3 Sep 2025. The issuer app may apply its own daily limits.
- **QRIS Tap (NFC):** the BI standard for tap-to-pay through licensed PJP apps on NFC phones. It follows the same nominal limit. Adoption is still small: about 634 thousand transactions in May 2026.
- **QRIS Antarnegara (cross-border):** live in **Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore, Japan, South Korea (since 1 Apr 2026) and China (since 30 Apr 2026, via Alipay/UnionPay links)**. BI is targeting India and Saudi Arabia next. Most use is inbound tourists paying Indonesian merchants with their home apps, which matters for Bali and tourist retail.

## 4. Identity, tax IDs and business documents

### 4.1 Personal and tax identifiers

- **NIK (Nomor Induk Kependudukan):** 16 digits, printed on the **KTP** (e-KTP) and on the **KK (Kartu Keluarga)**. KK has its own 16-digit KK number. Treat NIK, KTP photos and KK as sensitive personal data under UU PDP 27/2022 [UNVERIFIED for this pass]: never send them in group chats or store them unencrypted.
- **NPWP:** since **1 Jul 2024** (PMK 112/2022 as amended by PMK 136/2023):
  - resident individuals use their **NIK as NPWP**
  - companies (badan), non-resident individuals and government agencies use a **16-digit NPWP** (the old 15-digit number becomes 16 with a leading 0 [UNVERIFIED format detail])
- **NITKU (Nomor Identitas Tempat Kegiatan Usaha):** replaces "NPWP cabang" for each business location. It is 22 digits (NPWP 16 + 6-digit location code) [22-digit length UNVERIFIED in a fetched source; DJP guide linked]. It is used on faktur pajak and bukti potong for branch locations. Tax administration runs on **Coretax** (DJP).
- **NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha):** the business licence number from OSS (oss.go.id) [UNVERIFIED for this pass, needed for QRIS bank merchant onboarding and PIRT/halal etc.].

### 4.2 VAT (PPN) and local tax on documents

- **PPN:** statutory 12%, but for non-luxury goods and services the DPP (dasar pengenaan pajak) = 11/12 × price, so the **effective rate is 11%** (PMK 131/2024, since 1 Jan 2025; faktur code 04). Example: price Rp1.000.000 → DPP Rp916.667 → PPN Rp110.000.
- Only **PKP (Pengusaha Kena Pajak)** may issue a faktur pajak. Registering as PKP is mandatory once omzet exceeds Rp4,8 miliar per year [UNVERIFIED for this pass, PMK 197/2013 threshold].
- **Restaurants and cafés do not charge PPN.** They charge regional **PBJT makanan/minuman** (formerly "pajak restoran"/PB1) of **max 10%**, set by each Perda (UU HKPD 1/2022), and regions set their own omzet threshold. The service charge is separate from tax. PBJT is calculated on the bill after discount/service charge as the Perda defines.

### 4.3 Commercial documents: what and when

| Document                              | Purpose / when used                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Penawaran harga** (quotation)       | Seller to buyer before an order: items, price, validity period, payment terms.                                      |
| **PO (Purchase Order)**               | Buyer's formal order. Common with corporate/B2B buyers.                                                             |
| **SPK (Surat Perintah Kerja)**        | Work order for services or projects: scope, timeline, value. Signed before work starts.                             |
| **Kontrak / perjanjian**              | Larger or recurring deals. Often needs meterai.                                                                     |
| **Invoice / tagihan**                 | Payment request after delivery or at a milestone: invoice number, date, due date, bank details.                     |
| **Faktur pajak**                      | VAT invoice, issued only by PKP via Coretax/e-Faktur. B2B buyers need it to credit input VAT.                       |
| **Kwitansi**                          | Receipt confirming money was received (with "terbilang" amount, signature).                                         |
| **Nota / struk**                      | Simple sales receipt (retail/F&B). "Nota kontan" = cash sale, "nota kredit" = credit sale.                          |
| **Surat jalan / delivery order (DO)** | Goes with the goods. The recipient signs on arrival as proof of delivery.                                           |
| **BAST (Berita Acara Serah Terima)**  | Formal handover of goods or project results. Often a precondition for invoice payment by corporates and government. |
| **Berita acara** (other)              | Formal minutes of an event (e.g. stok opname, pemeriksaan).                                                         |

- **Payment terms norms [UNVERIFIED, common practice]:**
  - B2B: **tempo 30 hari** (net 30), also 14/45/60, counted from invoice or BAST.
  - Projects and custom orders: **DP (uang muka) 30–50%** with the balance (pelunasan) on delivery.
  - Large corporates and government often pay only after BAST + invoice + faktur pajak are complete.
  - Retail and online sales are paid in full upfront.
- **Bea meterai:** a single rate of **Rp10.000** (UU 10/2020). It is required on civil documents, including documents stating receipt of money **of more than Rp5.000.000** (i.e. > Rp5 juta, not ≥; exactly Rp5.000.000 is not subject to it). It also applies to contracts and agreements regardless of value. Use a physical meterai tempel or an **e-Meterai** (buy from official PERURI/DJP distributors) on PDFs.

## 5. Communications norms

### 5.1 Channel and tone

- **WhatsApp is the primary customer channel** for Indonesian SMBs: orders, booking (clinics), payment confirmation and complaints. Instagram DM and marketplace chat come second [general practice; no 2026 survey figure sourced → UNVERIFIED].
- **Forms of address:**
  - **Bapak/Ibu** (Pak/Bu) is the safe formal default for adults and B2B.
  - **Kak/Kakak** is the friendly, gender-neutral default for online shops and younger customers.
  - Mas/Mbak (Java), Bang/Uda/Uni (Sumatra, region-dependent) and Bli/Mbok (Bali) are local alternatives [UNVERIFIED regional detail].
- Avoid "kamu/lo" with customers. Use "Anda" in formal writing; in chat, "Kakak/Bapak/Ibu" usually replaces "Anda".
- Typical message shape: greeting (Selamat pagi/siang/sore/malam; "Assalamu'alaikum" if the customer uses it), name, polite request (mohon, silakan, terima kasih), and closing (Salam, [Nama toko]). Apologise readily ("Mohon maaf atas ketidaknyamanannya").
- **Response time [UNVERIFIED, expectation, not sourced]:**
  - Customers expect replies within minutes during business hours, ideally < 1 hour.
  - Send an auto-reply / away message outside hours with the opening hours and timezone.
- **Sending-hours etiquette [UNVERIFIED, practice]:**
  - Send business-initiated messages about 08.00–20.00 local time.
  - Avoid Friday about 11.30–13.00 (Jumatan) for Muslim audiences.
  - In Ramadan, avoid the buka puasa time (about 17.30–19.00); sahur-time messages are acceptable only for opted-in promos.
  - Do not send on Nyepi in Bali.
  - Use the recipient's time zone.

## 6. Logistics

- **Couriers (regular/express, nationwide):** JNE (REG/YES/OKE/JTR cargo), J&T Express (also J&T Cargo), SiCepat, Anteraja, Pos Indonesia, Ninja Xpress, ID Express, Lion Parcel, TIKI, SPX (Shopee) [service names partly UNVERIFIED].

- **Pickup cut-offs (aggregator Biteship):**
  - J&T: order by **15.59** for same-day pickup.
  - SiCepat: by **14.59**.
  - J&T Same Day service cut-off: about 10.00 (KiriminAja).
  - Times are local. Other couriers are typically early-to-mid afternoon [UNVERIFIED].
- After the cut-off, pickup moves to the next day. Most couriers deliver on Saturdays, and Sunday coverage is limited [UNVERIFIED].
- Shipping is weighed by actual or volumetric weight (common divisor 6.000 for cm³ → kg) [UNVERIFIED]. Every parcel gets a **resi** (tracking number). "Ongkir" = shipping cost. "Cek ongkir" = rate check.
- **Address format (order):**
  1. Recipient name + phone (mandatory for couriers)
  2. Jl. [street] No. [x], [building/blok]; Gg. for alleys
  3. **RT xx / RW yy**
  4. **Kelurahan** (urban) or **Desa** (rural)
  5. **Kecamatan**
  6. **Kota** or **Kabupaten**
  7. **Provinsi**
  8. **Kode pos** (5 digits)

## Likely to change / verify yearly

1. **SKB 3 Menteri holidays and cuti bersama.** The 2028 list is usually signed around September of the prior year. Islamic dates move after each Sidang Isbat. Revisions do happen.
2. **Ramadan / Idul Fitri dates:** final only after the Sidang Isbat (Feb–Mar 2027). Muhammadiyah may differ by a day.
3. **THR SE Menaker:** re-issued every year, usually in early Ramadan.
4. **QRIS MDR:** changed in Mar 2025 and again in Oct 2026, so BI may adjust it again. Also watch the QRIS nominal limit, QRIS Tap rollout and new cross-border countries (India, Saudi Arabia targeted).
5. **BI-FAST fee cap (Rp2.500):** BI has said it wants to lower it toward Rp0.
6. **Meta WhatsApp pricing:** changed Jul 2025 and Oct 2026, and rates per country can change quarterly.
7. **PLN tariffs:** reviewed every quarter (tariff adjustment).
8. **Gateway fees (Midtrans/Xendit):** list prices change without notice.
9. **PPN rate mechanics (12% with DPP 11/12)** and the PKP threshold. Also Coretax procedures, which are still evolving.
10. **PBJT rates and thresholds per Perda,** plus regional holidays (Bali facultative days, local anniversaries).
11. **Courier Lebaran schedules and cut-off times:** announced every year.
12. **Bea meterai rate and threshold:** stable since 2021, but tied to the law.
