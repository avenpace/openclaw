## 0. READ FIRST: the legal landscape changed in October 2026

- **Constitutional Court ruling (Putusan MK No. 168/PUU-XXI/2023, read 31 Oct 2024).** The Court struck down or reinterpreted 21 articles of the labour cluster of UU 6/2023 (Cipta Kerja). It ordered parliament to write a separate labour law. Points that matter to SMBs:
  - A PKWT may last **no more than 5 years including extensions**.
  - Employers must offer a weekly rest option of **2 days for a 5-day week** (alongside 1 day for a 6-day week).
  - Outsourcing types must be set by the Minister, with written agreements.
  - Wage structures must be proportional (position, tenure, education, competence).
  - A dismissal (PHK) that the worker disputes cannot take effect until a dispute-resolution body has ruled, after bipartite talks fail.
  - Foreign workers may be hired only with priority given to Indonesian workers.
- **New law passed: UU Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan.** DPR approved it at the 9th plenary session on **6 October 2026**: 23 chapters, 313 articles. Kemnaker (Dirjen PHI Indah Anggoro Putri) says it takes effect **one year after it is enacted and promulgated** ("satu tahun sejak ditetapkan, diundangkan"). The new minimum-wage provisions are expected to apply first to **UMP 2028**.
  - **[UNVERIFIED]** As of 1 Nov 2026 no research source confirmed the law number (UU No. __/2026), its promulgation date or its exact transitional provisions.
  - Reported key changes, all **[UNVERIFIED] until the official text is published**:
    - PKWT capped at **4 years**: an initial contract of up to 2 years plus up to 2 extensions of 1 year each.
    - Outsourcing restricted to 4–5 support job types: cleaning, catering, security, drivers, plus mining/oil-gas support (sources differ on the count).
    - The **0.5× severance multiplier is abolished**; the minimum multiplier becomes 1×.
    - A new **dana cadangan pesangon** (severance reserve fund), with details to come by PP.
    - Menstrual leave without a doctor's letter.
    - Employers may not hold workers' diplomas or certificates.
    - Apprenticeships limited to 6 months.
    - Minimum-wage alpha range 0.5–1.0, plus a BPS survey of KHL (decent living needs).
- **Practical rule for the assistant.** Until the new UU takes effect (expected around late 2027), the operative rules for day-to-day questions are still **UU 13/2003 as amended by UU 6/2023, read with MK 168/2023**, plus **PP 35/2021, PP 36/2021 (amended by PP 51/2023 and PP 49/2025)** and the BPJS regulations. Always add: "a new labour law (UU Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan) was passed on 6 Oct 2026 and will change contracts, outsourcing and severance once it takes effect. Check whether it already applies." _(Reading of the transition: the law reportedly replaces PP 35/2021 once in force. During the one-year transition the existing rules continue. This is an inference from the ministry's statements. **[UNVERIFIED]** pending the official transitional article.)_

## 1. BPJS Kesehatan (JKN health insurance)

**Rule.** Every employer must register all its workers and their families in JKN and pay contributions monthly. Workers are PPU (Pekerja Penerima Upah) participants.

**Figures**

| Item                                                          | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contribution                                                  | **5% of monthly wage**: **4% employer**, **1% employee** (deducted from pay)                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Wage base                                                     | Gaji pokok + tunjangan tetap                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Upper cap                                                     | **Rp12,000,000/month**. Above this the contribution is calculated on Rp12 million, so the maximum is Rp600,000/month (Rp480,000 employer + Rp120,000 employee)                                                                                                                        |
| Lower floor                                                   | The applicable UMK (or UMP where there is no UMK)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Covered by the 5%                                             | Worker + spouse + up to **3 children** (the standard family package; total of 5 people)                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Additional family (4th child onward, parents, parents-in-law) | **1% of wage per person per month**, paid by the worker                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Payment deadline                                              | **By the 10th of each month**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Late payment                                                  | No interest on the contribution. Coverage is suspended while in arrears. If the member needs inpatient care within **45 days** of reactivation, a service penalty applies: **5% of the initial inpatient diagnosis cost × months in arrears (max 12 months), capped at Rp30,000,000** |
| Admin sanctions for non-registration                          | Written warning, fine, and refusal of certain public services (PP 86/2013)                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

**KRIS status.** Perpres 59/2024 introduced the **Kelas Rawat Inap Standar (KRIS)**, a single standard inpatient room class replacing classes 1/2/3. As of August 2026 it was being rolled out in stages (Health Minister: phased over about 2 years).

- **No new contribution tariff has been set.** PPU contributions remain 5% (4/1), and the government said there would be no JKN contribution increase in 2026.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** Whether a new post-KRIS tariff will take effect in 2027.

**Legal basis:** UU 40/2004 (SJSN); UU 24/2011 (BPJS); Perpres 82/2018 on Jaminan Kesehatan as amended by Perpres 64/2020, 63/2022 and 59/2024; PP 86/2013 (sanctions).

**Registration.** Employers register via BPJS Kesehatan's employer channel (e-Dabu / Badan Usaha), linked to OSS/NIB. **[UNVERIFIED]** The exact deadline for registering a new hire (commonly cited as within 30 days).

**Worked example (bakery, Surabaya, gaji pokok + tunjangan tetap Rp5,500,000):**

- Employer 4% = Rp220,000
- Employee 1% = Rp55,000
- Total due by 10th = Rp275,000
- If the worker adds a mother as a dependant: an extra 1% = Rp55,000, deducted from the worker.
- Manager earning Rp15,000,000: base capped at Rp12,000,000, so employer Rp480,000 and employee Rp120,000.

## 2. BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (employment social security)

**Rule.** Employers must enrol workers in the social security programmes and pay monthly.

- **JKK** (work accident) and **JKM** (death) are mandatory for all employers.
- **JHT** (old-age savings) and **JP** (pension) are mandatory for medium and large enterprises. Micro and small enterprises must enrol at least in JKK, JKM and JHT; JP can be added **[UNVERIFIED: the exact current staging rule for micro/small]**.
- **JKP** (job-loss) is automatic for workers enrolled in the other programmes and in JKN.

**Contribution figures (percent of monthly wage = gaji pokok + tunjangan tetap)**

| Programme                          | Employer                                                                   | Employee | Total                                                              | Cap                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| JHT (Jaminan Hari Tua)             | 3.7%                                                                       | 2%       | 5.7%                                                               | No cap                                                                       |
| JKK (Jaminan Kecelakaan Kerja)     | 0.24% / 0.54% / 0.89% / 1.27% / 1.74% by risk class (very low → very high) | 0        | 0.24–1.74%                                                         | No cap                                                                       |
| JKM (Jaminan Kematian)             | 0.30%                                                                      | 0        | 0.30%                                                              | No cap                                                                       |
| JP (Jaminan Pensiun)               | 2%                                                                         | 1%       | 3%                                                                 | **Wage cap Rp11,086,300/month from 1 March 2026** (was Rp10,547,400 in 2025) |
| JKP (Jaminan Kehilangan Pekerjaan) | 0 extra                                                                    | 0 extra  | 0.36%, funded by 0.14% recomposed from JKK + 0.22% from government | Benefit wage cap Rp5,000,000                                                 |

- **JKK class** is assigned per business type by BPJS, not chosen freely. Most clinics, retail shops and services fall in very low or low risk (0.24% / 0.54%). **[UNVERIFIED]** The exact class for a given KBLI code.
- **JKK discount.** A 50% discount for designated labour-intensive industries (textiles, footwear, furniture, food and beverage manufacturing, etc., with ≥50 workers) under PP 7/2025 and its amendment ran until **January 2026**. **[UNVERIFIED]** Whether it was extended past January 2026. It never applied to ordinary clinics, retail or services.
- **JP wage cap formula.** The 2025 cap × (1 + GDP growth for 2025): Rp10,547,400 × 1.0511 = Rp11,086,372, rounded to **Rp11,086,300**. Set in BPJS TK letter **No. B/1226/022026 (25 Feb 2026)**. It is adjusted every March.
- **JKP benefits** (PP 37/2021 as amended by **PP 6/2025**, effective 7 Feb 2025):
  - Cash of **60% of the reported wage for 6 months**, using a wage cap of Rp5,000,000 (so max Rp3,000,000/month).
  - Job-market information and job training.
  - Eligibility: an Indonesian citizen (WNI), enrolled in JKN and BPJS TK, on PKWTT or PKWT, who was dismissed (PHK).
  - Not available on resignation, total permanent disability, retirement or death.
  - PP 6/2025 relaxed the earlier contribution-history requirement. **[UNVERIFIED]** The exact remaining minimum contribution period and the claim deadline (formerly 12 months of contributions within 24 months, 6 consecutive; claim within 3 months of PHK).

**Deadlines and penalties**

- Pay by the **15th of the following month**.
- Late payment: **2% denda per month** on the arrears (JKK, JKM, JHT; PP 44/2015, PP 46/2015).
- JP late payment: **0.5% per month** under PP 45/2015. **[UNVERIFIED]** This was not re-confirmed online this session; BPJS's own article speaks only of 2%.
- If the employer is in arrears when an accident happens, the employer may be liable for benefits.
- Escalation runs: inspection → referral to Kejaksaan (as state attorney) → litigation.
- Admin sanctions (PP 86/2013): warning, fine, and refusal of public services such as permits and tenders.

**Registration.** Via OSS/NIB integration (company and worker data synchronise to BPJS TK), the **SIPP Online** employer portal, or a branch office. Employers must report wage changes and new hires and exits monthly.

**Legal basis:** UU 40/2004; UU 24/2011; PP 44/2015 (JKK/JKM, as amended incl. PP 82/2019); PP 46/2015 (JHT, as amended by PP 60/2015); PP 45/2015 (JP); PP 37/2021 as amended by PP 6/2025 (JKP); PP 7/2025 and its amendment (JKK discount); PP 86/2013 (sanctions).

**Worked example (same Surabaya bakery worker, Rp5,500,000, JKK very low 0.24%):**

|                   | Employer         | Employee       |
| ----------------- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| JHT               | 3.7% = Rp203,500 | 2% = Rp110,000 |
| JKK               | 0.24% = Rp13,200 | –              |
| JKM               | 0.30% = Rp16,500 | –              |
| JP                | 2% = Rp110,000   | 1% = Rp55,000  |
| **BPJS TK total** | **Rp343,200**    | **Rp165,000**  |

Adding BPJS Kesehatan (Section 1): **employer total Rp563,200**, **employee deduction Rp220,000**.

Manager on Rp15,000,000:

- JP is capped at Rp11,086,300, so employer 2% = Rp221,726 and employee 1% = Rp110,863.
- JHT is uncapped: 3.7% = Rp555,000 and 2% = Rp300,000.

## 3. Minimum wage (Upah Minimum) 2026

**Rule.**

- Employers may not pay below the applicable minimum wage. The UMK applies where one is set; otherwise the UMP applies.
- Minimum wage is a **safety net for workers with less than 12 months' service**. Longer-serving workers must be paid under the company's **struktur dan skala upah**.
- Some regions also set sectoral minimums (UMSK).
- **Effective 1 January 2026.**

**2026 regulation and formula.**

- Basis: **PP 49/2025**, the second amendment of PP 36/2021 on Pengupahan, signed 17 Dec 2025.
- Formula: **inflation + (economic growth × alpha)**, with **alpha widened to 0.5–0.9** (previously 0.1–0.3), chosen by the Provincial Wage Council (Dewan Pengupahan).
- Governors had to announce the UMP by 24 Dec 2025.
- Example from Makassar: UMK 2025 Rp3,880,136 + [2.61% inflation + (5.39% growth × 0.8)] = +6.92%, giving Rp4,148,719.

**Micro and small enterprises (Usaha Mikro dan Kecil).** Under PP 36/2021 art. 36 (as amended), minimum wage provisions **do not apply** to micro and small enterprises. Their wages are set by **agreement** between employer and worker, subject to two floors:

- at least **50% of the average household consumption** in the province, and
- at least **25% above the provincial poverty line**.

Micro/small status follows PP 7/2021 criteria: micro means business capital up to Rp1 billion or turnover up to Rp2 billion; small means Rp1–5 billion capital or Rp2–15 billion turnover. **[UNVERIFIED]** The exact Rupiah floors for 2026. ## 4. Working time and overtime (PP 35/2021)

**Rule.**

- Normal hours are **40 hours/week**: either **7 hours/day × 6 days** or **8 hours/day × 5 days**.
- At least 30 minutes' rest after 4 consecutive hours of work.
- Weekly rest is 1 day (6-day week) or 2 days (5-day week), reconfirmed by MK 168/2023.
- Some sectors may use different schemes set by the Minister.

**Overtime (lembur)**

- Needs a written order and the worker's consent; a list signed by both is acceptable.
- Maximum **4 hours/day and 18 hours/week**. Days off and public holidays are excluded from these limits.
- The employer must pay overtime, give an opportunity to rest, and provide food and drink of at least 1,400 kcal if overtime is 4 hours or more.
- Workers in certain job groups (high responsibility, flexible hours) with a higher wage may be excluded from overtime pay if agreed in the PK, PP or PKB.

**Hourly rate.** Upah sejam = **1/173 × monthly wage**.

- Base = gaji pokok + tunjangan tetap.
- If the pay also contains non-fixed allowances and pokok + tetap is below 75% of total pay, the base is 75% of total pay.

**Multipliers**

| Situation                                                                         | Multiplier        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Working day: 1st overtime hour                                                    | 1.5 × hourly rate |
| Working day: each subsequent hour                                                 | 2 ×               |
| Rest day/public holiday, **6-day week**: hours 1–7                                | 2 ×               |
| same: hour 8                                                                      | 3 ×               |
| same: hours 9–11                                                                  | 4 ×               |
| same, if the holiday falls on the shortest working day: hours 1–5 / 6th / 7th–9th | 2 × / 3 × / 4 ×   |
| Rest day/public holiday, **5-day week**: hours 1–8                                | 2 ×               |
| same: hour 9                                                                      | 3 ×               |
| same: hours 10–12                                                                 | 4 ×               |

**Legal basis:** UU 13/2003 art. 77–78 and 85 as amended by UU 6/2023; PP 35/2021 art. 21–32.

**Worked example (retail clerk, monthly Rp5,500,000 pokok + tetap):**

- Hourly rate = 5,500,000 / 173 = **Rp31,791.91**.
- 3 hours overtime on a weekday: (1.5 + 2 + 2) × 31,791.91 = **Rp174,855**.
- 10 hours on a Sunday (5-day-week shop): (8×2 + 1×3 + 1×4) = 23 × 31,791.91 = **Rp731,214**.

## 5. THR (Tunjangan Hari Raya Keagamaan)

**Rule.**

- Every worker with **at least 1 month of continuous service** is entitled to THR once a year, for their own religious holiday. This covers PKWT and PKWTT, permanent and contract, including harian lepas.
- THR must be paid **in full, not in instalments**, no later than **7 days before the holiday (H-7)**.

**Formula**

| Service                           | THR                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ≥12 months continuous             | 1 month's wage (gaji pokok + tunjangan tetap, or the net wage if paid all-in) |
| 1 to <12 months                   | (months of service / 12) × 1 month's wage                                     |
| Harian lepas, ≥12 months          | Average monthly wage over the last 12 months                                  |
| Harian lepas, <12 months          | Average monthly wage over the months worked                                   |
| Piece-rate / output-based workers | Average over the 12 months before the holiday                                 |

- If the PK, PP, PKB or custom gives more, the higher amount applies.
- A PKWTT worker dismissed within 30 days before the holiday is still entitled. A PKWT whose term ends before the holiday is not entitled.

**Late payment.**

- A **5% denda** of the total THR, paid into a fund for worker welfare. The denda does not remove the duty to pay the THR.
- Administrative sanctions: warning → restriction of business activity → suspension of production means → freezing of business (PP 36/2021).

**2026 specifics.**

- Circular: **SE Menaker No. M/3/HK.04.00/III/2026**, dated 3 March 2026.
- Idul Fitri 1447 H was set by the government's sidang isbat at **Saturday 21 March 2026** (Muhammadiyah: 20 March). The THR deadline was therefore **Saturday 14 March 2026**.
- For Christmas 25 Dec 2026, the THR deadline is **18 Dec 2026**. Nyepi was 19 Mar 2026 and Waisak 31 May 2026. Each worker's THR follows their own religion's holiday unless agreed otherwise.

**Bonus vs THR.** THR is mandatory. A bonus is discretionary, depends on company profit and the PK/PP/PKB, and cannot replace or be netted against THR.

**Legal basis:** PP 36/2021 art. 8–9 and 62, 79 (as amended); Permenaker 6/2016 on THR Keagamaan; the yearly SE Menaker.

**Worked example (clinic nurse, joined 1 August 2025, wage Rp5,000,000, Lebaran 21 March 2026):**

- Completed months to the holiday: Aug–Feb = 7 (plus 20 days).
- THR = 7/12 × 5,000,000 = **Rp2,916,667**, due by 14 March 2026.
- Day-counting conventions vary: some employers count partial months. Use the PP/PKB rule if one exists.

## 7. Employment contracts

**PKWT (Perjanjian Kerja Waktu Tertentu, fixed-term)**

- Only for work that is temporary, seasonal, a new product/activity in trial, or expected to finish within a set time. **Not for permanent core work.**
- Must be **in writing**, in Indonesian and Latin script.
- **Maximum 5 years including extensions** (PP 35/2021 art. 8, affirmed by MK 168/2023).
  - Under the new UU, reportedly 2 years + 2 × 1-year extensions = 4 years max **[UNVERIFIED, not yet in force]**.
- **No probation allowed**. A probation clause in a PKWT is void and the service still counts.
- A PKWT that breaks the rules (wrong type of work, no written form, over the limit) becomes PKWTT by law.
- **Registration:** the employer must register the PKWT online with Kemnaker **within 3 working days of signing**. If the online system is unavailable, register in writing with the Disnaker kabupaten/kota within 7 working days.
- **Compensation (uang kompensasi)** at the end of a PKWT for workers with **at least 1 month** of service (PP 35/2021 art. 15–16):
  - 12 months continuous = 1 month's wage.
  - Less than 12 months = (months / 12) × 1 month's wage.
  - More than 12 months = (months / 12) × 1 month's wage.
  - Paid at the end of each PKWT, including before an extension.
  - Not payable to foreign workers (TKA).
  - Example: an 18-month PKWT at Rp5,000,000 → 18/12 × 5,000,000 = **Rp7,500,000**.
- **Early termination** of a PKWT by either party: the terminating party pays the other the wages for the remaining term, unless the PKWT says otherwise. **[UNVERIFIED]** Whether this art. 62 UU 13/2003 rule survives unchanged in the new UU.

**PKWTT (Perjanjian Kerja Waktu Tidak Tertentu, permanent)**

- May be oral, but the employer must then issue a letter of appointment (surat pengangkatan).
- **Probation (masa percobaan) max 3 months**, only for PKWTT.
- Wages during probation may not be below the minimum wage.

**Daily casual workers (pekerja harian lepas)**

- Allowed for work that varies in time and volume, with pay based on attendance.
- **Fewer than 21 days per month.** If a worker works **21 days or more for 3 consecutive months or more**, the arrangement converts to PKWTT (PP 35/2021 art. 10).
- The employer must keep a written list of daily workers.
- Daily workers are still owed THR and BPJS coverage.

**Outsourcing (alih daya)**

- Under PP 35/2021 the vendor must be a licensed legal entity, and workers' protection, wages and severance are the vendor's responsibility.
- **Permenaker 7/2026** (issued 29 April 2026) limits outsourced _work types_ to support work: cleaning; food and beverage provision for workers; security; drivers/worker transport; operational support services; and support work in mining, oil-gas and electricity.
  - It requires written agreements and gives companies **2 years** to adjust.
  - A revision cutting this to 4 types (catering, security, drivers, cleaning) was announced for July 2026. **[UNVERIFIED]** Whether it was issued.
- The new UU also narrows outsourcing and gives outsourced workers explicit severance rights once in force.
- **Practical point for SMBs:** do not "outsource" core staff such as cashiers, nurses or bakers to avoid employment obligations.

**Legal basis:** UU 13/2003 art. 56–66 as amended by UU 6/2023; MK 168/2023; PP 35/2021 art. 2–20 and 18–20 (outsourcing); Permenaker 7/2026.

## 8. Termination (PHK) and severance (PP 35/2021)

**Process rule.**

- Employer, worker and union should try to prevent PHK.
- If PHK is intended, the employer gives the worker a written notice with reasons, **at least 14 working days in advance** (7 working days during probation).
- If the worker rejects it, a **bipartite negotiation** follows, then mediation/conciliation at Disnaker, then the Pengadilan Hubungan Industrial.
- Under MK 168/2023, a contested PHK is final only after the dispute body's ruling. Wages and obligations continue during the process.

**Wage base:** gaji pokok + tunjangan tetap (the last monthly wage).

**Uang Pesangon (UP), PP 35/2021 art. 40(2)**

| Service   | Months' wages |
| --------- | ------------- |
| < 1 year  | 1             |
| 1 to < 2  | 2             |
| 2 to < 3  | 3             |
| 3 to < 4  | 4             |
| 4 to < 5  | 5             |
| 5 to < 6  | 6             |
| 6 to < 7  | 7             |
| 7 to < 8  | 8             |
| ≥ 8 years | 9             |

**Uang Penghargaan Masa Kerja (UPMK), art. 40(3)**

| Service        | Months' wages |
| -------------- | ------------- |
| 3 to < 6 years | 2             |
| 6 to < 9       | 3             |
| 9 to < 12      | 4             |
| 12 to < 15     | 5             |
| 15 to < 18     | 6             |
| 18 to < 21     | 7             |
| 21 to < 24     | 8             |
| ≥ 24 years     | 10            |

**Uang Penggantian Hak (UPH), art. 40(4)**

- Unused annual leave not yet lapsed.
- Travel costs home for the worker and family to the place of hire.
- Other rights in the PK, PP or PKB.
- _(The old 15% housing/medical component of UU 13/2003 no longer applies under UU 6/2023 / PP 35/2021.)_

**Uang pisah** (separation pay) is set in the PK, PP or PKB. It applies to workers not entitled to UP/UPMK, such as those who resign.

**Multipliers by reason (PP 35/2021 art. 41–59).** These still apply until the new UU takes effect. The new UU reportedly removes all 0.5× multipliers.

**Worked example (bakery supervisor, 6 years 3 months' service, wage Rp6,000,000, PHK for efficiency to prevent losses):**

- UP: 7 months × 1 = Rp42,000,000
- UPMK: 6 to <9 years → 3 months = Rp18,000,000
- UPH: say 4 unused leave days. The daily-wage divisor is per company policy; commonly 1/25 (6-day week) or 1/21 (5-day week) of monthly pay **[UNVERIFIED as a legal rule]**.
- **Total ≈ Rp60,000,000 + UPH.**
- If the PHK were for efficiency _due to losses_: UP 0.5× = Rp21,000,000 + UPMK Rp18,000,000 = Rp39,000,000 + UPH.

**Resignation entitlements.**

- UPH (unused leave and so on) + uang pisah as per the PK/PP/PKB.
- No pesangon or UPMK.
- JHT can be claimed after the worker's account becomes inactive. **[UNVERIFIED]** The current waiting period (1 month under Permenaker 4/2022).
- **No JKP on resignation.**
- A PKWT worker who resigns early may owe the remaining-term compensation (see Section 7).

## 9. Payroll practicalities

**Slip gaji.** The employer must give a wage slip (bukti pembayaran upah) each payday, showing the components and deductions (PP 36/2021). Wages are paid at least monthly, in Rupiah.

**Late wages.** If the employer pays late, a denda applies:

- 5% per day of delay from day 4 to day 8 after the due date;
- then an additional 1% per day;
- capped at 50% of the wage for a month.

**[UNVERIFIED]** That this PP 36/2021 art. 61 schedule is unchanged.

**Typical order of computation**

1. Gross wage = gaji pokok + tunjangan tetap + tunjangan tidak tetap + overtime.
2. Employee social-security shares, from the capped bases where applicable:
   - BPJS Kesehatan 1% (cap Rp12 million);
   - JHT 2% (no cap);
   - JP 1% (cap Rp11,086,300);
   - plus 1% for each additional family member.
3. **PPh 21** withheld by the employer.
   - Since Jan 2024 monthly withholding uses the **TER (Tarif Efektif Rata-rata) tables** (PP 58/2023, PMK 168/2023), applied to monthly gross income by PTKP category (TER A/B/C).
   - December uses the annual Article 17 calculation, which allows deduction of biaya jabatan (5%, max Rp6 million/year), employee JHT/JP contributions and PTKP.
   - **2026 incentive:** **PPh 21 DTP** (government-borne) for workers earning up to Rp10 million/month in designated labour-intensive industries (footwear, textiles/garments, furniture, leather) and **tourism** (hotels, restaurants/cafés and similar), for January–December 2026, under PMK 10/2025 as amended (PMK 105/2025 and PMK 72/2025).
   - **[UNVERIFIED]** The exact KBLI eligibility list. A bakery café or restaurant may qualify under tourism KBLI; a clinic or ordinary retail generally does not.
4. Other agreed deductions such as loans or cooperative dues: only with written agreement, and total wage deductions are limited to **50% of the wage** (PP 36/2021).
5. Net pay.

Note that the employer's BPJS shares (4% health, 3.7% JHT, JKK, JKM, 2% JP) are paid on top of gross wages. For PPh 21, the employer's JKK, JKM and BPJS Kesehatan premiums count as taxable income of the employee (natura/benefit in gross income). **[UNVERIFIED]** Treatment detail; confirm with a tax consultant.

**WLKP (Wajib Lapor Ketenagakerjaan Perusahaan)**

- Every company must report its workforce data **every year** (and when it opens, moves or closes) via **WLKP Online**, integrated with OSS/NIB, Dukcapil and the BPJS TK membership number.
- Basis: UU 7/1981; Permenaker 18/2017 as amended by Permenaker 4/2019.
- In Oct 2026 Kemnaker required WLKP registration for companies joining the MagangHub programme and opened 3 complaint centres.

**Minimum age**

- Working age is **18**.
- Children aged **13–15** may do only light work: max 3 hours/day, during the day, not during school hours, with written parental consent and wages paid. Basis: UU 13/2003 art. 68–69.
- Ages 15–18 must not do the worst forms of child labour, including hazardous work or night work.

**Foreign workers (TKA), basics**

- Need an approved **RPTKA** (plan for foreign worker use) via the TKA Online system.
- Pay the **DKP-TKA** levy of **US$100 per position per month** **[UNVERIFIED for 2026]**.
- Appoint an Indonesian counterpart (tenaga kerja pendamping) and provide training.
- TKA may not hold personnel/HR positions and may not be hired on PKWT for permanent work.
- Enrol them in BPJS if working in Indonesia for 6 months or more.
- Basis: UU 6/2023 art. 42–49; PP 34/2021. MK 168/2023 requires prioritising Indonesian workers.

## 10. What the assistant must NEVER do

1. **Never give final legal advice on a dispute** (PHK contested, unpaid wages claims, PKWT-to-PKWTT conversion claims, union matters, harassment or discrimination complaints, accidents with injury).
   - Give the general rule and a rough calculation clearly labelled as an estimate.
   - Then direct the owner to the **Dinas Tenaga Kerja (Disnaker) kabupaten/kota** for mediation, a **labour lawyer**, or a licensed HR/payroll consultant.
   - For BPJS disputes, direct them to the BPJS branch office.
2. **Never advise evading obligations.** This includes:
   - paying below UMK without micro/small status;
   - fake "freelance", "partner" or outsourcing labels for core staff;
   - not registering BPJS, or under-reporting wages to BPJS;
   - splitting THR into instalments;
   - holding a worker's diploma or ID;
   - firing a pregnant worker or a worker on maternity leave;
   - backdating contracts.
3. **Never state a figure as certain if it is marked [UNVERIFIED] here.** Never invent a UMK, KBLI class or tax rate. Say "check the Kepgub / BPJS / DJP" instead.
4. **Always flag the October 2026 law change** when answering about PKWT duration, outsourcing or severance multipliers, and ask the owner to confirm whether UU Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan has taken effect at the time of the question.
5. **Never decide on a worker's personal claims** (JHT, JKP, BPJS benefits) on their behalf. Point them to the official channels: the JMO app or a BPJS TK branch, and the Mobile JKN app.
6. **Do not process or store** workers' sensitive data (NIK, health data) beyond what the owner's task needs. Remind owners of UU 27/2022 (Pelindungan Data Pribadi).

## Likely to change / verify yearly

- **UMP/UMK**: re-set every year, announced by late December and effective 1 January. UMP 2027 is due around Dec 2026; the new-law formula is expected from UMP 2028.
- **JP wage cap**: adjusted every **March** using GDP growth (2026: Rp11,086,300 from 1 Mar 2026).
- **BPJS Kesehatan tariff after KRIS**: there was no change in 2026; watch for a new Perpres in 2027.
- **UU Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan** (passed 6 Oct 2026):
  - the law number, promulgation date and effective date (about 1 year later);
  - new PKWT limits (4 years reported), outsourcing scope, removal of 0.5× pesangon, severance reserve fund;
  - implementing PPs that will replace PP 35/2021 and amend PP 36/2021;
  - a separate law on platform workers due within 2 years.
- **Outsourcing Permenaker 7/2026** and its announced revision (4 job types).
- **THR**: the yearly SE Menaker and that year's Idul Fitri date (sidang isbat).
- **SKB 3 Menteri**: public holidays and cuti bersama for the next year (usually issued in September–November).
- **JKK 50% discount** and **PPh 21 DTP** incentives: time-limited (DTP runs through Dec 2026).
- **JKP eligibility details** under PP 6/2025, and any further amendment.
- **PPh 21 TER tables / PTKP**: check DJP each January.
- **DKP-TKA levy** and TKA procedures.
- **Micro/small wage floors** (provincial consumption and poverty-line data from BPS).
