## 1. Clinic types, ownership, personnel, permits, accreditation

### Rules

- **Klinik Pratama** gives basic (primary) medical services. A clinic may be "pratama umum", "pratama gigi" or "pratama umum dan gigi". **Klinik Utama** gives specialist services. Under Permenkes 11/2025 private clinics are classed as klinik pratama, klinik utama or klinik pratama pendukung (e.g. occupational clinics). Inpatient limits: pratama at most 5 days, utama at most 7 days.
- **KBLI** 86105 (private clinic). OSS rates it a medium-high risk activity, so the licence is an NIB plus a **Sertifikat Standar** that must be verified. The old Dinkes "Izin Operasional Klinik" has been absorbed into the OSS-RBA licence (sometimes called "PB Klinik"). Issuance takes up to 25 days (one source says working days) after the documents are declared complete.
- **Who may own:** for an outpatient clinic, individuals (perorangan) or business entities. Inpatient clinics must be a business entity. Foreign-owned (PMA) clinics must be a PT and are verified centrally. **[UNVERIFIED: perorangan vs badan usaha detail, from izin.co.id and medminutes; the Lampiran of Permenkes 11/2025 was not read directly]**
- **Prerequisites (Permenkes 11/2025 Pasal 8(3)):** KKPR (spatial-use conformity), persetujuan lingkungan, and PBG plus SLF for the building.
- **Personnel:**
  - **Kepala Klinik:** a tenaga medis (dokter or dokter gigi), Indonesian citizen, with an active SIP at that clinic.
  - **Penanggung jawab pelayanan:** at a pratama, a dokter, a family-medicine/primary-care doctor or a dokter gigi (dokter gigi for a dental clinic). At an utama, a specialist or subspecialist (dokter gigi spesialis for a dental utama).
  - **Minimum doctors at a pratama:** 2 dokter for pratama umum; 2 dokter + 1 dokter gigi for pratama umum dan gigi; **2 dokter gigi for pratama gigi**. **[UNVERIFIED: from secondary summaries; confirm in the Lampiran]**
  - **Pharmacy:** an apoteker is required if the clinic runs pharmacy services (see §7).
- **Building and equipment:** permanent building, not physically joined to a private home. Some public areas may be shared if the organisation is separate. Minimum room sizes apply (exam room about 7.2 m² is cited). Outpatient clinics open at least 8 hours a day; inpatient clinics run 24 hours with 5–20 beds. Dental needs: a separate extraction/minor-surgery area and sterilisation (autoclave) to standard. **[UNVERIFIED: dental room specifics and hour minimums are from Permenkes 17/2024 summaries; Permenkes 11/2025 may carry them over]**
- **Accreditation (Permenkes 34/2022):** mandatory. The first accreditation must happen within **2 years of the licence**. Status lasts **5 years**. Apply for reaccreditation at least 3 months before expiry. Surveys are run by a lembaga penyelenggara akreditasi that the Ministry designates (KMK HK.01.07/MENKES/32/2023), against standards in KMK 1983/2022. Accreditation is also required to contract with BPJS. Falsely claiming accreditation is sanctioned (Pasal 33).
- **Post-licence:** register on the Kemenkes Registrasi Fasyankes system for a kode fasyankes, implement an RME integrated with SATUSEHAT, and report routinely.

### Procedure (new clinic or renewal check)

1. Settle the entity (perorangan, PT perorangan, PT) and obtain NPWP and NIB on oss.go.id under KBLI 86105.
2. Secure KKPR, the environmental document (SPPL or UKL-UPL), and PBG/SLF that match a health use.
3. Upload the clinic profile, org chart, self-assessment against the Permenkes 11/2025 standard, STR/SIP of all staff, equipment and drug lists, and the B3 waste contract (MoU with a licensed transporter or processor).
4. Dinkes verifies (visit or virtual), then the Sertifikat Standar is issued.
5. Register the fasyankes code, register with SATUSEHAT (§3), and apply for accreditation before the 2-year mark.

## 2. Dentist and dental-therapist practice

### Rules

- **STR (Surat Tanda Registrasi):** under UU 17/2023 it is **valid for life** (STR seumur hidup). Registration is now handled by the **Konsil Kesehatan Indonesia (KKI)**, which answers to the Minister under UU 17/2023 (the old independent Konsil Kedokteran Indonesia was folded in). Practitioner status can be checked on the SATUSEHAT SDMK profile pages. **[UNVERIFIED: exact current STR application portal]**
- **SIP (Surat Izin Praktik):** one SIP covers one practice location (PP 28/2024 Pasal 682(2)). A dokter or dokter gigi may hold at most **3 SIPs (3 locations)**. The SIP is issued by the regency/city government (Dinkes or DPMPTSP). It is **valid 5 years** and is renewed when SKP (Satuan Kredit Profesi) adequacy is met. SIPs issued before the law stay valid until they expire.
- **SKP:** in Sept–Oct 2026 the Ministry cut the SKP requirement for doctors from 250 to 160 per 5 years. **[UNVERIFIED: the figure for dokter gigi, which one source gives as 100; the regulation number behind the 2026 change]**
- **Terapis Gigi dan Mulut (TGM)** (Permenkes 20/2016; replaced the old "perawat gigi" title). TGM need a SIPTGM for each location, at most 2 under that Permenkes **[UNVERIFIED: whether PP 28/2024 changed the count for tenaga kesehatan]**. Their own authority covers dental health promotion, prevention, service management and limited basic care: extracting deciduous teeth and single-root permanent teeth under local anaesthesia, and one- or two-surface fillings (e.g. GIC). Dental assisting is allowed inside a facility only. Multi-surface fillings, root canal treatment and medication are allowed **only on written delegation (pelimpahan wewenang) from a dentist**.
- **Tukang gigi** are not tenaga kesehatan. Kemenkes has issued no new tukang gigi licences in about 23 years. They must not be presented as clinic clinicians.

### Procedure (assistant)

- Keep a register of each clinician's STR number, SIP number, location and **SIP expiry**. Alert 6 months before expiry.
- Before scheduling a new dentist, confirm they hold a SIP _for this clinic's address_ and fewer than 3 SIPs in total.

## 3. Medical records (Rekam Medis) and SATUSEHAT

### Rules

- **Electronic medical records are mandatory** for every fasyankes, including independent dentist practices and clinics. The deadline was **31 Dec 2023** (Permenkes 24/2022 Pasal 3, 45). Enforcement runs through SE HK.02.01/MENKES/1030/2023, which sets administrative sanctions. In 2026 the Ministry sanctioned 1,306 hospitals; clinic-specific enforcement figures were not found. **[UNVERIFIED: how SE 1669/2025 and the March 2026 SE apply to clinics as opposed to hospitals]**
- **Sanctions** (Pasal 42–43): written warning, then a recommendation to revoke accreditation or revocation of it. They are triggered by complaints or monitoring.
- **Content:** recording must be complete and clear, made after the patient is served, and carry the provider's name, time and signature (Pasal 16(2)). It includes administrative and clinical data. Dental records conventionally include an **odontogram** **[UNVERIFIED: no primary source found that makes the odontogram mandatory under Permenkes 24/2022]**. Discharge or summary documents must contain identity, findings, diagnosis/treatment and the provider's signature.
- **Retention:** at least **25 years from the patient's last visit** (Pasal 39). After that, records may be destroyed unless still needed, following the destruction procedure. Prescriptions are kept at least 5 years (Permenkes 34/2021).
- **Ownership:** the **document belongs to the fasyankes** (Pasal 25; UU 17/2023 Pasal 297(1)). The **content belongs to the patient** (Pasal 26).
- **Patient access:** patients may receive a resume or summary and a copy, printed or electronic. A representative needs specific written authority; for a deceased patient, proof of family relationship and a death certificate. A reasonable copy fee is common practice, but records should not be withheld for non-payment. **[UNVERIFIED: the fee and withholding points come from a secondary legal guide]**
- **Confidentiality and disclosure:** health secrets are protected by UU 17/2023 Pasal 301ff. Disclosure **without consent** is limited to Pasal 305 grounds: law enforcement (e.g. court order), ethics or discipline proceedings, the patient's own interest, public health (outbreaks) and other grounds the law names. Permenkes 24/2022 Pasal 33–37 sets the access procedure; some purposes (e.g. research, audit) need approval. Health data is also **specific (sensitive) personal data** under **UU 27/2022 PDP**. Never disclose to an employer, insurer, spouse or partner without the patient's specific written consent. The one exception is BPJS or an insurer under a claims contract, and then only as that contract requires.
- **Corrections:** a provider may correct their own clinical entries (Pasal 16(4)). Errors must **never be deleted**. In paper records the error is struck through so it stays legible and is initialled. In RME the original stays in the audit trail with who, when and why. The facility head assigns role-based access rights for input, correction and view (Pasal 30). **[UNVERIFIED: a "48-hour correction window" reported by one summariser of Pasal 30; confirm before relying on it]**
- **SATUSEHAT:** the RME **must connect to and interoperate with** the Kemenkes platform (Pasal 21). Referrals transfer through the platform (Pasal 24). The RME vendor (PSE) must register the system with the Ministry (Pasal 12), and the facility must open RME access to the Ministry (Pasal 28). Minimum outpatient data: **Encounter** (visit), **Condition** (diagnosis, ICD-10 WHO 2010 version, one code per payload), **Observation** (vital signs) and **Procedure**. Medication data follows as integration matures. Hospitals sending under 50% of Encounter/Condition data have been flagged for accreditation adjustment. A similar metric for clinics is **[UNVERIFIED]**.

### Procedure: SATUSEHAT onboarding

1. Obtain the **kode fasyankes** through Registrasi Fasyankes (https://registrasifasyankes.kemkes.go.id).
2. Register on the SATUSEHAT Platform (start at https://link.kemkes.go.id/integrasiSATUSEHAT). You receive an Organization ID and API credentials (client ID/secret), first for sandbox, then production after approval.
3. Use an RME listed on https://satusehat.kemkes.go.id/platform/system-rme-list, or have your own system pass integration testing.
4. Send Encounter, Condition, Observation and Procedure for every visit, and monitor the send rate in the SATUSEHAT dashboard.

## 4. Informed consent (Persetujuan Tindakan)

### Rules (UU 17/2023 Pasal 293; Permenkes 290/2008 operationally)

- **Every** health service action needs the patient's consent after an adequate explanation. The explanation covers diagnosis, indication, the procedure, risks and complications, alternatives and prognosis.
- Consent may be oral or written. **Written consent is required for invasive or high-risk actions.** In dentistry that typically means extractions (especially surgical or impaction), minor oral surgery, endodontics under anaesthesia and implants. Treat any procedure under local anaesthesia as written-consent by default **[UNVERIFIED: the dental list is practice guidance, not statute text]**.
- A competent patient consents personally. If the patient is not competent, a representative consents.
- **Written consent is signed by the patient or representative and witnessed by a tenaga medis or tenaga kesehatan** (Pasal 293(8)).
- **Minors and incompetent patients:** consent comes from a parent or guardian. Permenkes 290/2008 Pasal 13–14 gives the family order (spouse, parents, adult children, siblings). The age of adulthood for consent is **[UNVERIFIED]**: Permenkes 290/2008 uses "dewasa" (historically 21 or married), while UU 17/2023 and child-protection law treat under-18s as children. The assistant should default to a parent or guardian for anyone under 18 and flag the case to the dentist.
- **Refusal** must be documented in writing and signed (Permenkes 290/2008 Pasal 16).
- **Emergency:** if no one can consent, the action proceeds in the patient's best interest as decided by the clinician (Pasal 293(9)–(10)).
- Consent forms are part of the rekam medis and are retained with it.

## 5. BPJS Kesehatan (JKN) for dental

### Rules

- **FKTP types** include Puskesmas, praktik dokter, **praktik dokter gigi**, **Klinik Pratama** and RS D Pratama. A private klinik pratama can be an FKTP after **kredensialing**. Credentialing criteria are HR, facilities and service scope, and **accreditation** is required. Rekredensialing is periodic, done by BPJS together with Dinkes.
- **Kapitasi** (Permenkes 3/2023, the latest tariff found). Klinik pratama: Rp9,000–16,000 per member per month, depending on doctor ratio, whether a dentist is available, and performance (kapitasi berbasis kinerja). Praktik mandiri dokter gigi: Rp3,000–4,000 per member per month. Dental service at a klinik pratama is funded inside its kapitasi; one source quotes Rp11,000–12,000 for clinics with a dentist **[UNVERIFIED]**. A new JKN tariff and contribution increase was planned for 2026 **[UNVERIFIED whether it has been issued as of Nov 2026]**.
- **Covered at FKTP (within kapitasi):** examination, treatment and consultation; premedication; extraction of deciduous teeth (topical or infiltration); uncomplicated extraction of permanent teeth; post-extraction medication; fillings (tumpatan); scaling **on medical indication** (e.g. gingivitis), never on request or for cosmetics.
- **Scaling frequency:** recent sources (CNBC Jun 2026, Detik) say **once every 2 years**; older sources say once a year **[UNVERIFIED: confirm against the current BPJS benefit guideline]**.
- **Dentures (protesa gigi):** paid as a **non-capitation claim**. At FKTP the cap is Rp1,000,000 for both jaws (Rp500,000 per jaw); at FKRTL Rp1.1M (Rp550,000 per jaw). Replacement interval **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Not covered:** cosmetic and aesthetic dentistry, orthodontics (braces) and services without medical indication. **[UNVERIFIED: exhaustive exclusion list; Perpres 82/2018 as amended, e.g. by Perpres 59/2024]**
- **Referral:** specialist dental care is at FKRTL only, through an (online) FKTP referral, and only on medical indication. Dental referrals are made by the dentist. Non-specialist cases should be handled at FKTP; excess non-specialist referrals hurt the KBK score.
- **Claims:** kapitasi is paid monthly per registered member. Non-capitation claims (e.g. protesa) are submitted to the BPJS branch. Encounter data also flows to SATUSEHAT and P-Care.

### Procedure (assistant)

- Check the patient's JKN status (active, no arrears) and that they are **registered to this clinic** before treating them as BPJS.
- Record the medical indication for scaling or protesa, and record the date so the frequency limit can be checked.
- Never charge a BPJS patient for a covered service (iur biaya) unless a BPJS rule allows it. If the patient wants a non-covered upgrade, get written agreement on the price first.

## 6. Pricing, patients' rights, emergencies, complaints

### Rules

- **Patient rights** (UU 17/2023 Pasal 276): information about their own health; adequate explanation of services; care that meets standards; the right to consent or refuse; confidentiality; and access to their own records.
- **Price transparency:** patients are entitled to an explanation that includes an estimate of cost before treatment. Practical rule: publish a tariff list at the front desk and give a written estimate before any non-trivial treatment. **[UNVERIFIED: no clinic-specific article mandating a posted tariff list was found; Permenkes 4/2018 on patient rights is RS-focused]**
- **Emergencies** (UU 17/2023 Pasal 174): in an emergency a fasyankes **must not refuse the patient, ask for a deposit (uang muka), or put administration ahead of care**. **Pasal 438** sets criminal penalties for failing to give first aid (triage plus appropriate action), heavier if disability or death results. For a dental clinic this means: stabilise (bleeding, airway, anaphylaxis after anaesthesia), then refer. Never turn someone away at the door for money.
- **Complaints:** have an internal complaint channel and log, which is also an accreditation item. Patients may also complain to Dinkes. Professional-discipline complaints go to the Majelis Disiplin Profesi (MDP) set up under UU 17/2023, and ethics complaints to PDGI. Disputes should first go to mediation or out-of-court settlement. **[UNVERIFIED: the article numbers for the MDP and complaint procedure]**

## 7. Medical waste, X-ray radiation safety, pharmacy

### Medical waste (Limbah B3 medis)

- **Basis:** PP 22/2021 (B3 waste), Permen LHK P.56/2015 (B3 waste from fasyankes) and Permen LHK 6/2021 (general B3 procedures).
- **Steps:** reduce and segregate at source (infectious, sharps, pathological, pharmaceutical, chemical; dental amalgam and X-ray fixer are B3 chemical waste). Then store in a TPS for at most **2 days above 0 °C** or **90 days at or below 0 °C**. Then hand over to a transporter **licensed for B3 waste** and onward to a licensed processor (incinerator or autoclave plant). Keep the contract (MoU), the manifests (the electronic manifest system, Festronik, is run by KLHK **[UNVERIFIED for clinic use]**) and processor receipts. The B3 MoU is an OSS licensing document.
- Sharps go into puncture-proof safety boxes. Never put B3 waste in municipal rubbish.

### Dental X-ray (BAPETEN)

- Intraoral, panoramic and CBCT units are ionising-radiation sources and need a **BAPETEN utilisation licence** (izin pemanfaatan tenaga nuklir) **before use**. Since 2025 it is issued through **OSS**, valid **5 years**. Service time is about 5 working days for intraoral units, 10 for other diagnostic radiology.
- Three core documents: the facility technical document, the radiation protection and safety programme, and the management system document. A **Petugas Proteksi Radiasi (PPR)** certified by BAPETEN must be appointed. Also required: annual safety verification reports, equipment maintenance, and the BAPETEN regulation on diagnostic and interventional radiology (Perba 2020). Personal dosimetry and conformity testing (uji kesesuaian) are normally required **[UNVERIFIED: specific clause]**.
- Radiology services in clinics also follow **Permenkes 24/2020** (clinical radiology service standards).

### Pharmacy and drug storage (Permenkes 34/2021)

- An outpatient clinic need **not** run pharmacy services. If it does, it must have an **apoteker** (with SIPA) as penanggung jawab, who may be assisted by Tenaga Teknis Kefarmasian. If not, the clinic may cooperate with another clinic or an apotek (Pasal 3(5)). Inpatient clinics must have an Instalasi Farmasi with an apoteker.
- Keep **prescriptions at least 5 years**. Submit pharmacy reports to the district/provincial Dinkes. Narcotics and psychotropics need special locked storage and reporting (general BPOM/Kemenkes rules). Storage temperature, FEFO and expiry control apply. Local anaesthetic cartridges (e.g. lidocaine with epinephrine) are prescription drugs; keep a stock log. **[UNVERIFIED: specific clause numbers for storage]**

## 8. Tax specifics

### Rules

- **PPN:** medical health services (jasa pelayanan kesehatan medis) moved from the non-taxable list to **"dibebaskan"** (exempt facility) under UU HPP (UU PPN Pasal 16B(1a)(j)) and **PP 49/2022 Pasal 10**. The exemption covers services of dokter gigi and dokter gigi spesialis, and of klinik and FKTP. **Cosmetic or beauty-clinic services may be subject to PPN** (DJP's Kring Pajak has said so). Teeth whitening and other purely aesthetic dental work: ask the tax adviser **[UNVERIFIED]**. Because the services are exempt, input PPN on equipment generally cannot be credited.
- **PPh 21 for non-employee doctors (dokter mitra)** (PMK 168/2023): the clinic withholds **Pasal 17 rates × 50% of gross**. Gross means the full doctor's fee paid by patients through the clinic, **before** the clinic's share (bagi hasil) is deducted. This is calculated per payment or month, not cumulatively. Issue a bukti potong each month (through Coretax). The doctor credits it in their annual SPT.
- **PPh 21 for employees** (salaried dentists, nurses, admin): monthly TER (tarif efektif rata-rata), with a December true-up (PMK 168/2023).
- **PPh final 0.5% (UMKM):** **PP 20/2026** (amends PP 55/2022) limits it to **individuals, PT Perorangan and koperasi** with turnover of at most Rp4.8 billion. Ordinary **PT, CV and Firma are no longer eligible** (transitional use through tax year 2026 **[UNVERIFIED]**). Income from **pekerjaan bebas, including doctors' professional fees, is excluded**. A dentist's own fee is never PPh-final income. A clinic run by an individual or PT Perorangan may use the 0.5% on _business_ turnover only if it qualifies. **[UNVERIFIED: PP 20/2026 issue date (sources say 22 Apr or 2 Jun 2026); how "klinik" business income vs doctors' fees is split for an owner-dentist]**
- Doctors keeping norma (NPPN) instead of books need the turnover under the threshold (Rp4.8B) and must notify DJP. Ask the tax adviser.

## 9. Advertising and promotion

### Rules

- **Permenkes 1787/MENKES/PER/XII/2010** (Iklan dan Publikasi Pelayanan Kesehatan, still in force). Ads must be informative, educational, accurate, evidence-based and not misleading. **Prohibited:** patient **testimonials** in ads or publications; **superlative or "the only/best" claims**; **discounts (diskon) or rewards** for health services; false or misleading superiority claims; promoting unproven methods. **Health professionals may not model in ads** for drugs, devices or facilities, except public service announcements (Pasal 8).
- **KODEKGI** (Kode Etik Kedokteran Gigi Indonesia, PDGI, 2020 edition): no excessive self-promotion or self-praise on social media, no endorsing products, and no curating patient praise for public posting. Before/after photos used as promotion are widely treated as violations. Ethics breaches go to the PDGI ethics council (MKEKG).
- **What is fine:** the clinic name, address, hours, services offered, practitioner names, qualifications and SIP, a factual price list (not "promo"), and genuine educational content.

### Procedure (assistant)

- Before posting anything: no testimonials, no before/after, no "best/cheapest/painless guaranteed", no discount campaigns, no dentist in product ads. If the owner insists, state the rule and leave the decision with the owner and the dentist.

## 10. Hard rules for the AI assistant (never do)

1. **Never diagnose, prescribe, or recommend a treatment or drug dose.** Clinical questions from patients go to the dentist. The assistant may share general clinic information (hours, prices, preparation instructions the dentist has approved).
2. **Never create, edit or delete rekam medis content.** Only the treating dentist or authorised staff may correct entries, through the RME amendment flow: the original is kept and the change is logged with who, when and why. Deletion is never allowed.
3. **Never disclose a patient's data** (identity, visit, diagnosis, photos, X-rays, bills) to anyone other than the patient. That includes family, an employer, an insurer, other patients, the owner's friends, social media and journalists. The exceptions are the patient's own **specific written authorisation**, or a lawful request under UU 17/2023 Pasal 305 handled by the clinic head. Health data is sensitive data under UU PDP.
4. **Never confirm whether a particular person is a patient** to a third party.
5. **Never turn away or delay an emergency for payment or paperwork.** Escalate to the dentist at once and advise calling 119 or going to the nearest IGD.
6. **Never quote BPJS coverage as certain** where this file says [UNVERIFIED]. Check P-Care or the BPJS branch.
7. **Never publish promotional content** that breaks §9.
8. **Never let a clinician without a valid SIP for this location be scheduled.** Flag SIP and accreditation expiries.
9. Tax, legal and licensing answers are a starting point. Tell the owner to confirm with the tax adviser, Dinkes or a lawyer before acting.

Sources: §3, §4, §6, §9 above; UU 27/2022 PDP.
